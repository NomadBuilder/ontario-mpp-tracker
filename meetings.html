How this works

  1. Twice a day we open each place’s public meeting calendar — the same one the clerk posts for residents.
  2. We list upcoming council, planning, and committee meetings so people can show up or request a deputation.
  3. When an agenda is posted, we read it for data-centre language and related terms, then mark those cards green.
  4. Every card links back to the official agenda. This is a heads-up, not a replacement for the municipality’s site.

What “exact” and “broad” mean

  • Exact — the agenda or title actually says data centre / data center / datacentre.
  • Broad — that, plus things cities often use instead: hyperscale, colocation, AI campus, named operators (Equinix, Prologis, and others), large-load or IESO connection studies, crypto mining.

We match words on the page. A rezoning that never uses any of that language will stay a plain “scan agenda” card until someone reads it.

How often

About 9:15am and 9:15pm Eastern, every day. Places marked “checked twice a day” are in that round. Others are on our list but not in the automatic pass yet.
