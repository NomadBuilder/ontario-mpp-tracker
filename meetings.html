How this works

  1. Twice a day we open each place’s public meeting calendar — the same one the clerk posts for residents.
  2. We list upcoming council, planning, and committee meetings so people can show up or request a deputation.
  3. When an agenda is posted, we read it for data-centre and water-privatization language (and related terms), then mark those cards.
  4. Every card links back to the official agenda. This is a heads-up, not a replacement for the municipality’s site.

What the filters mean

  • Data centres — exact — the agenda or title actually says data centre / data center / datacentre.
  • Data centres — broad — that, plus hyperscale, colocation, AI campus, named operators, large-load / IESO connection, crypto mining.
  • Water privatization — selling, privatizing, or handing the municipal water system to a private operator.
  • Water — broad — that, plus P3/PPP water deals, outsourcing water services, Veolia / EPCOR / similar, bulk water sales, water franchises.

We match words on the page. A rezoning or utility file that never uses any of that language will stay a plain “scan agenda” card until someone reads it.

How often

About 9:15am and 9:15pm Eastern, every day. Places marked “checked twice a day” are in that round. Others are on our list but not in the automatic pass yet.
